    ENGINEERING FORMULAS

    Calculation Method

    Connected Load

    Pconnected = N × Pcharger

    Total rated power of all chargers.

    Design Load

    Pdesign = Pconnected × DF

    Preliminary diversified load.

    3-Phase Current

    I = P / (√3 × V × PF)

    Estimated AC input current.

    Apparent Power

    S = P / PF

    Used for preliminary transformer sizing.

    Voltage Drop

    ΔV ≈ √3 × I × R × L

    Simplified resistive approximation.

    Battery Duration

    t = E / P

    Approximate full-power duration.
